# Grey Door Republik
*Independent Games for Strange Places.*

### About the Studio

Grey Door Republik is an independent game development studio based in the United Kingdom, specialising in unusual interactive experiences, unconventional storytelling, and games that explore the curious space between the familiar and the unsettling.

Founded by a solo developer with a professional background in multimedia design, interactive learning, and digital experiences, the studio brings together years of creative and technical expertise with a longstanding fascination for retro technology, forgotten systems, and the peculiar logic of everyday institutions.

Grey Door Republik was established with a straightforward ambition: to create distinctive games that don't necessarily follow established conventions. Games that reward curiosity, encourage exploration, and occasionally leave players wondering whether they've discovered something they weren't supposed to.

### Our Approach

The studio takes inspiration from an eclectic collection of influences: early home computing, analogue technology, institutional architecture, bureaucratic absurdity, and the wonderfully imperfect character of software from another era.

Rather than pursuing technological spectacle, Grey Door Republik focuses on atmosphere, originality, and purposeful design. Limitations are embraced as creative opportunities, with an emphasis on making modest resources produce unexpectedly rich experiences.

As a deliberately small studio, every project is developed with a personal approach to design, storytelling, and technical execution. The objective isn't to make the biggest games. It's to make games that remain interesting long after they've been switched off.

### Our First Project: Floppy//144

The studio's debut project, **Floppy//144**, embodies this philosophy.

Built around the constraints and aesthetics of obsolete computing technology, Floppy//144 invites players into a peculiar institutional world of records, procedures, forgotten information, and quietly unsettling discoveries.

Blending exploration, environmental storytelling, and an intentionally retro presentation, the game celebrates an era when developers had to achieve remarkable things within remarkably small technical limits.

It is both a tribute to those constraints and an experiment in what can still be accomplished by embracing them.

### Looking Ahead

Grey Door Republik is an independent creative venture with an open-minded approach to future projects. Whether exploring strange fictional institutions, experimenting with forgotten technologies, or developing entirely new interactive worlds, the studio remains committed to making games with personality, imagination, and a healthy disregard for the ordinary.
